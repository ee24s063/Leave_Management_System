Monthly Leave
  • Every employee gets 2.5 days when they are added, and 2.5 more on the 1st of every month.
  • Unused days carry over to the next month, and into the next year.
  • All leave types (Casual, Medical, ...) take their days from this one balance. Compensatory leave has its own balance.
  • To change someone's balance, use Adjust Balance below.

Remaining = monthly days added + adjustments/credits - used. Balances can go below zero. Balances are not tied to a year: unused days carry over and never expire.

Adjust Balance

Add days (e.g. 2) or remove days (e.g. -1.5) for the employee selected above, for corrections or working-day adjustments. Every adjustment is recorded in the balance history.
